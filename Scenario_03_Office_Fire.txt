# Scenario 03: Office Building Fire Drill

**Purpose:** Test evacuation decision-making in a commercial/office setting.

### Caller script

1. "The fire alarm is sounding on the 4th floor of my office building."
2. "There is light haze and smoke in the main corridor."
3. "Can I take the elevator down quickly?"
4. "The designated fire stairs are clear on the north side."
5. "I have evacuated to the outdoor assembly point with the fire wardens."

### Expected agent behaviour

- Instruct caller never to take elevators during a fire alarm.
- Direct caller to use clear, designated fire escape stairs.
- Advise staying low if smoke is present.
- Remind caller to follow directions from floor fire wardens.
- Confirm caller reaches the outdoor assembly point safely.

### Evaluation focus

Prompt evacuation, elevator prohibition, following warden instructions, and reaching designated safe zone.
