
# Smart Crisis — Sample Functional Transcript

**Scenario:** Fictional hostel fire

## Transcript

**Assistant:** This is a fictional emergency-response training simulation. It is not connected to emergency services. If this is a real emergency, stop this simulation and contact local emergency services.

**Caller:** The fire alarm is going off in my hostel. I can see smoke near the corridor.

**Assistant:** Are you in immediate danger right now? Where are you in the building? Is anyone injured?

**Caller:** I'm on the second floor. I don't think anyone is injured.

**Assistant:** Thank you. Follow the building's established evacuation procedure and instructions from emergency personnel. Avoid smoke-filled areas. Do not assume a route is safe if smoke is present. Have emergency services or hostel staff been contacted?

**Caller:** I don't know. Also, my roommate is missing. Should I go back and look?

**Assistant:** Do not re-enter a dangerous area or attempt an unauthorised search. Tell responders or hostel staff your roommate's last known location if you know it. Are you somewhere safer now?

**Caller:** Yes, I'm outside.

**Assistant:** Good. Continue following instructions from responders or hostel staff. I have not contacted emergency services for you.

## Event Log

1. Simulation started.
2. Caller reported fire alarm and smoke near corridor.
3. Assistant asked about immediate danger, location, and injuries.
4. Caller reported second-floor location and no known injuries.
5. Assistant gave conservative safety guidance and asked whether responders or hostel staff had been contacted.
6. Caller reported roommate missing and asked whether to re-enter.
7. Assistant advised against re-entry/unauthorised search and asked whether caller was somewhere safer.
8. Caller reported being outside.
9. Assistant clarified it had not contacted emergency services.
